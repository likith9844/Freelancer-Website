1. Executive Summary & Core Product Principle

KaryaSetu is engineered as a two-sided professional freelance marketplace specifically customized for the Indian economy. Unlike simple job boards or basic e-commerce software, KaryaSetu facilitates bilateral legal agreements, multi-tier milestone escrow deposits, automated tax withholding under Indian Income Tax Section 194-O, GST invoice generation under SAC code 998314, and double-entry financial settlement via payment splitting rails.

Key Architectural Invariant: Zero client-side math is trusted. All platform fees, GST calculations, Section 194-O TDS, and ledger allocations are calculated exclusively server-side. Every financial movement generates an immutable 5-legged double-entry transaction record where Debits == Credits across the global general ledger.

1 Onboarding & Indian Identity Verification (KYC) Identity & Regulatory Layer

Clients register with business entity details (Company Name, GSTIN for B2B input tax credits, and Billing Address).
Freelancers complete a specialized Indian professional onboarding workflow:

  • PAN Validation: Verified against CBDT format ([A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]{1}) with 4th character checked (e.g., 'P' for Individual). Essential for Section 194-O TDS credit into Form 26AS.
  • Bank Penny-Drop Verification: NPCI IMPS ₹1 penny-drop validation matches beneficiary name on bank account against the legal PAN name.
  • Aadhaar Masking: In strict compliance with DPDPA 2023, Aadhaar is never stored in plaintext. Only masked representation (XXXX-XXXX-1234) is retained.
  • Razorpay Route Onboarding: A linked sub-merchant account (acc_...) is created to receive split payouts.
2 Project Publication & Smart Proposal Submissions Bidding & Scoping

The client posts a project specifying scope, required tech stack, estimated duration, and budget. Registered freelancers evaluate requirements and draft proposals itemized into discrete milestones.

Server-Side Tax Preview: Before submitting, the freelancer sees a live breakdown calculated against real-time admin settings: Gross Bid Amount − Platform Commission (e.g. 0% or configured %) − 18% GST on Commission − 1% Section 194-O TDS = Exact Net Take-Home Inflow.

3 Bilateral Contract & Digital Signature Execution Contract Formation

Upon selecting the winning freelancer, the platform compiles an immutable Bilateral Contract with a unique Indian registration identifier (e.g., KS-CTR-2026-001).

  • Contract legally binds Client, Freelancer, and Platform as Escrow Agent.
  • Platform commission and client fee rates are permanently locked into the contract record (protecting both parties against retroactive fee adjustments).
  • Bilateral digital signatures (SHA-256 fingerprint, user ID, IP address, timestamp) are recorded into the database before any escrow funding can commence.
4 Milestone Funding into RBI Nodal Escrow Financial Inflow

The client initiates milestone funding. Razorpay Standard Checkout opens supporting Indian payment methods: UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Net Banking across 50+ Indian banks, and Credit/Debit Cards.

The Client Funding Calculation (Server-Side Invariant): 
Milestone Consideration:           ₹50,000.00
+ Client Service Fee (3%):            ₹1,500.00
+ GST on Service Fee (18%):             ₹270.00
+ Gateway Interchange Charge (2%):    ₹1,035.00
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Total Client Inflow Payable:        ₹52,805.00
Double-Entry General Ledger Posting (Atomic 5-Legged Transaction):
  • DEBIT: 1010-GATEWAY-CLEARING ₹52,805.00 (Asset: Inflow receivable)
  • CREDIT: 2010-CLIENT-ESCROW ₹50,000.00 (Liability: Held in trust for milestone)
  • CREDIT: 4010-COMMISSION-REVENUE ₹1,500.00 (Revenue: Platform client service fee)
  • CREDIT: 2030-GST-PAYABLE ₹270.00 (Liability: Output GST on service fee)
  • CREDIT: 2020-GATEWAY-FEE-PAYABLE ₹1,035.00 (Liability: Gateway processing interchange)
Net Result: Total Debits (₹52,805) == Total Credits (₹52,805).

Upon payment gateway webhook confirmation (verified using HMAC-SHA256 signature verification), the milestone transitions to FUNDED, and an automated SMS notification is dispatched to the freelancer.

5 Work Execution, Deliverable Submissions & Revision Cycles Production Stage

The freelancer works in the encrypted project workspace. Upon completing work, the freelancer submits: Deliverable release notes, staging demo URLs, Figma links, and deliverable file archives. Milestone state updates to SUBMITTED.

The client examines the submission. The client has three options:

  1. Request Revision: Specific feedback provided. Milestone moves to REVISION_REQUESTED. Work continues until satisfactory.
  2. Open Milestone Dispute: Escrow is frozen immediately. Ticket is escalated to Support Admin arbitration.
  3. Approve Deliverable: Triggers instantaneous escrow release and tax withholding.
6 Milestone Approval, Tax Withholding & Split Payout Settlement Layer

When the client approves the deliverable, the platform server-side settlement engine executes:

The Freelancer Settlement Calculation (Example with 10% Platform Commission): 
Gross Milestone Escrow:             ₹50,000.00
- Platform Commission (10%):         -₹5,000.00
- GST on Commission (18%):             -₹900.00
- Section 194-O TDS (1% of Gross):     -₹500.00
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Net Withdrawable Payout:            ₹43,600.00
Note: If Admin sets Platform Commission to 0%, Commission is ₹0, GST is ₹0, and the freelancer takes home ₹49,500 (₹50,000 minus ₹500 mandatory TDS).

Razorpay Route Execution: If Route is enabled in live mode, a split transfer API call (POST /v1/transfers) automatically routes ₹43,600 directly to the freelancer's linked bank account.

7 Withdrawals & IMPS Bank Settlements Payout Operations

For wallet-mode payouts, freelancers request funds withdrawal (minimum ₹500). The Finance Admin executes an IMPS bank transfer. The platform records the unique 12-digit Bank Reference Number (UTR e.g., CMS2026101299001) and permanently deducts the balance.

8 Dispute Arbitration & Nodal Ledger Reversal Dispute Resolution

If a dispute arises, the Support Admin inspects deliverable artifacts, milestone specifications, and workspace chat logs. The admin renders a binding arbitration verdict: Client Full Refund, Freelancer Full Release, or Split Settlement (e.g. 60% client refund / 40% freelancer release). The ledger executes corresponding debit/credit reversals with zero financial leakage.

3. Will It Work in a Real-World Scenario? (Feasibility Analysis)

Yes, the core software architecture and financial engine will work in a real-world scenario. The software design adheres to real Indian statutory accounting principles, RBI banking guidelines, and double-entry ledger bookkeeping.

However, transitioning a freelance marketplace software from development to a legally licensed, commercially operating enterprise in India requires understanding four distinct operational pillars:

Operational Pillar How Current Architecture Solves It Required Live Real-World Step
1. Banking & Escrow Regulations (RBI Mandate) Platform implements full double-entry ledger tracking client escrow (Account 2010) separately from platform revenue (Account 4010). Funds do not mix. Under RBI Guidelines on Payment Aggregators & Gateways, client money cannot be deposited into a private company current account. You must operate via an RBI-compliant Nodal Escrow Account managed by a commercial bank or leverage Razorpay Route which operates on Razorpay's RBI PA license.
2. Income Tax Compliance (Section 194-O TDS) Calculates strictly 1% Section 194-O TDS on every milestone release, captures PAN, validates PAN format, and generates TRACES-compliant CSV data. Your company must obtain a TAN (Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number), deposit deducted TDS monthly via Challan ITNS 281, and file quarterly Form 26Q (Annexure III) returns so freelancers get tax credit in their Form 26AS.
3. Goods & Services Tax (GST SAC 998314) Platform charges 18% GST on platform commission and client service fees under SAC 998314. Computes CGST/SGST (intra-state) vs IGST (inter-state). Obtain GST Registration. Generate GST e-invoices with HSN/SAC 998314 and IRN for B2B corporate clients, and file monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B returns.
4. Data Privacy (DPDPA 2023) Aadhaar numbers masked (XXXX-XXXX-1234). OWASP rate limiting on auth, sanitization of user input, encrypted session tokens. Publish a comprehensive Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) compliant privacy policy with a designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) contact.

4. Production Implementation Blueprint (What Else is Needed)

To launch this platform into a commercial production environment with thousands of clients and freelancers, the following operational and architectural extensions must be provisioned:

A. Database & Infrastructure Migration (Production Tier) DevOps
  • PostgreSQL Database Migration: Migrate from local JSON storage to a high-availability Managed PostgreSQL cluster (e.g. AWS RDS Multi-AZ, Supabase, or Google Cloud SQL) with Prisma ORM connection pooling (PgBouncer).
  • S3-Compatible Cloud Storage: Connect Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2, or Google Cloud Storage with time-limited pre-signed URLs for portfolio items, deliverable ZIPs, and KYC identity proof uploads (never store binary uploads on app server disks).
  • Redis Caching & Queue Worker: Use Redis (Upstash / AWS ElastiCache) for distributed session tracking, rate limiting counters, and background job processing (BullMQ) for webhooks and invoice rendering.
B. Live Payment Rails & Account Onboarding (Razorpay Route) Fintech Operations
  • Razorpay Route Production Agreement: Apply for Razorpay Route specifically for marketplaces. Complete corporate KYC (Certificate of Incorporation, MOA/AOA, Audited Financials, Board Resolution).
  • Production Webhook Infrastructure: Deploy dedicated webhook listener with signature verification (HMAC-SHA256) and replay attack protection using idempotent event IDs.
  • Automated Sub-Merchant KYC: Integrate Razorpay Route Onboarding APIs so freelancers submit bank account and KYC documents directly through hosted partner onboarding flows.
C. Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) SMS DLT Compliance Regulatory Messaging
  • DLT Registration: Register your business entity on the TRAI DLT portal (via Vilpower, Jio, or Airtel).
  • Sender Header & Template Approval: Register a 6-character sender ID (e.g. KARYAS) and obtain regulatory approval for Transactional SMS templates for OTPs, milestone funding alerts, and dispute updates.
  • SMS Gateway Integration: Connect MSG91, Fast2SMS, or Twilio India with DLT template IDs.
D. Legal Contracts, NDAs & IP Assignment Framework Legal Architecture
  • Intellectual Property (IP) Assignment Clause: Include statutory clauses transferring full economic ownership and copyright of code, designs, and deliverables from the freelancer to the client immediately upon milestone escrow release.
  • Platform Non-Circumvention Policy: Establish legal penalties for clients and freelancers attempting to bypass platform escrow or solicit payments off-platform within 24 months of initial contact.
  • Standard Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA): Optional one-click mutual NDA execution attached to bilateral contracts.
E. Automated Fraud Prevention & Anti-Circumvention Filters Security & Moderation
  • Workspace Content Scanning: Heuristic regex scanning to detect exchange of personal contact information (Indian phone numbers, WhatsApp links, personal UPI handles, Telegram IDs) before contracts are digitally executed.
  • Transaction Anomaly Detection: Flagging suspicious rapid-cycle milestone releases, self-hiring patterns (same IP/device for client and freelancer), or abnormal invoice sums.
